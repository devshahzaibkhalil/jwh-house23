Chatbot Privacy Notice

This development notice explains the intended handling of information submitted through the James Wholesale Homes chatbot. Replace it with a professionally reviewed privacy policy before production launch.

Information collected

The chatbot may collect contact details, property information, buying or selling requirements, uploaded files, submitted links, callback preferences and conversation history.

Purpose

Information is used to review and respond to real estate enquiries, manage follow-up and protect the service against abuse.

Your controls

Users should be given a way to request correction or deletion of stored information. Marketing permission must remain optional and separate from permission to respond to an enquiry.
